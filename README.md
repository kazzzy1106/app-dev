# My Favorite Movie: How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days

**How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days** is one of my favorite romantic comedies because of its witty banter, chaotic premise, and fun New York setting.

> "How to lose a guy in 10 days!"

## Highlights & Why I Love It
- **Main Characters:** Andie Anderson and Benjamin Barry bring great comedic timing and chemistry.
- **Plot Premise:** Both leads enter the relationship with completely opposite secret agendas, making every scene unpredictable.
- **Vibe & Humor:** Lighthearted, fast-paced, and easy to rewatch anytime.

*A classic comfort movie for a fun watch!*
